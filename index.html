A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

What Mac do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS Tahoe 26 or later. This distribution doesn’t support Intel Macs, Windows or Linux.

Is the demo showing my Mac’s real readings?

No. The interactive preview uses simulated data in your browser. It doesn’t connect to your Mac’s hardware, processes, ports or containers.

Does Mac Gauge collect my information?

Mac Gauge does not collect telemetry or analytics. The direct-download build checks api.github.com for updates. The App Store build makes no update requests of its own; Apple handles its updates. The website’s demo uses fictional data.

How much will Mac Gauge cost?

Pricing is coming soon. The comparison above shows published paid competitor prices to help you explore the landscape.

Are all sensor readings available?

Availability depends on your hardware and macOS permissions. The sandboxed App Store or TestFlight build can have different access from the direct-download build.

Where are my menu bar icons?

On macOS 26, allow Mac Gauge in System Settings → Menu Bar. Also check that at least one module and widget is enabled in Mac Gauge.

Can I join TestFlight, and how do updates work?

Internal TestFlight testing is by invitation; there is no public invitation yet. For stable direct builds, install a newer DMG or run brew upgrade --cask mac-gauge if you installed through Homebrew.
